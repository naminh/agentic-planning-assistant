# Policy document licensing and attribution

Reviewed on 30 June 2026.

This notice covers the third-party policy documents under `data/local_policy` and `data/national_policy`. It does not change ownership of the source PDFs, and it does not apply to the sample material under `data/doncaster`.

## Open Government Licence v3.0

Where a file is identified below as OGL-covered, the required attribution is:

> Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

The full licence is available at <https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/>.

The OGL does not cover personal data, logos and crests, third-party rights that the information provider cannot license, trade marks, patents, or other excluded rights. Copyright and attribution notices embedded in the source PDFs continue to apply.
